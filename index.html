            How to sweep

            1. Hold the phone away from electronics for two seconds so it learns the room.
            2. Move it slowly, a hand's width from each object: smoke detector, clock, charger, bulb holder, hooks.
            3. The beeps speed up as the field bends. Past +25 µT it sounds the alarm: a powered device is right there.

            Chargers, speakers, laptops and steel also bend the field. When it alarms, point the Lens finder at the same spot to look for the lens.
